# Product Requirements Document (PRD): AirWise AI

- **Product Name:** AirWise AI
- **Tagline:** *Breathe Smarter. Live Cleaner. Act Together.*
- **Document Status:** Approved Baseline (v1.0)
- **Target Release Model:** Incremental 7-Phase Production Rollout

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

Severe winter smog and chronic urban air pollution affect hundreds of millions of residents, yet existing air-quality apps stop at showing raw AQI numbers or generic warnings. **AirWise AI** bridges the gap between raw environmental telemetry and daily household decision-making.

AirWise AI combines **verified multi-provider air quality and weather telemetry**, **deterministic environmental safety & impact engines**, **evidence-grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, and **conversational personalization** so individuals, families, and neighborhoods can reduce pollution exposure immediately while adopting practical, lower-emission routines over time.

### Foundational Product Distinction
Every screen, AI response, and metric in AirWise AI explicitly distinguishes three domains:
1. **Personal Exposure Reduction:** Actions that lower the concentration or duration of polluted air an individual inhales (highest priority).
2. **Emission Reduction:** Actions that reduce greenhouse gases or local exhaust/smoke generated by mobility and household energy.
3. **Community Impact:** Collective neighborhood actions that build awareness, curb local smoke/idling sources, and track voluntary participation—without falsely claiming an individual action measurably shifts city-wide AQI.

## 3. Functional Scope & Product Modules

### Module 1: Authentication, Sessions & User Governance
- **Capabilities:** Email/password registration & login, email verification, password reset, optional Google OAuth, profile & household preference management, active session inspection/revocation, JSON data export, and self-service account deletion.
- **Roles:** `Guest` (unauthenticated public AQI & educational access), `Registered User`, `Community Moderator`, `Administrator`.
- **Security Baseline:** Argon2id hashing, `HttpOnly` secure cookies, CSRF protection on state-changing endpoints, refresh-token rotation, and strict object-level RBAC.

### Module 2: Consent-First Location Intelligence
- **Location Input Modes:** Explicit opt-in browser geolocation, city search, postal code lookup, or manual map pin selection.
- **Saved Locations:** Multiple labeled locations (`Home`, `Workplace`, `School`, `Other`) storing display name, city/region/country, timezone, `location_precision` (`city`, `postal_code`, `neighborhood`, `exact`), and primary flag via PostGIS.
- **Graceful Degradation:** Denying geolocation keeps 100% of city-level features working; city-wide averages are never presented as street-level sensor readings.

### Module 3: Real-Time & Historical Air Quality Intelligence
- **Multi-Provider Ingestion:** Primary + configurable fallback adapter architecture (OpenAQ, OpenWeather Air Pollution, government/CPCB feeds, plus explicitly labeled `fixture` mode for offline local dev).
- **Pollutants Tracked:** `PM2.5`, `PM10`, `NO2`, `SO2`, `CO`, `O3` with concentration, canonical unit, measurement timestamp, provider, station/model provenance, and freshness status.
- **Isolated AQI Standards:** Dedicated calculators for **US EPA AQI** and **India National AQI (NAQI)**. Never mix breakpoints or category names; return `unavailable` when required averaging windows or pollutants are missing.
- **Visualizations:** Current AQI card, 24h / 7d / 30d trend charts (only showing ranges backed by actual data), forecast strip, station map, and WCAG AA non-color-only severity badges.

### Module 4: Hybrid Personalized Recommendation Engine
- **Inputs:** Consented household profile (age bracket, schedule, commute modes, cooking/ventilation setup, purifier availability, budget tier, sustainability goals) + live/forecast AQI & weather + RAG guidelines.
- **5 Recommendation Domains:**
  1. *Daily Routine Optimization* (measured/forecast windows rather than static morning/evening myths)
  2. *Low-Emission Transportation* (evaluating exposure safety before emissions savings)
  3. *Indoor Air Quality Improvements* (HEPA sizing, source control, wet cleaning, window timing; zero houseplant/ozone claims)
  4. *Household Sustainability* (clean cooking, appliance efficiency, waste-burning prevention)
  5. *Community Actions* (school no-idling zones, neighborhood awareness, incident reporting)

### Module 5: AirWise Copilot (Conversational GenAI Assistant)
- **Controlled Tool-Calling:** Access strictly limited to 12 RBAC-gated backend tools (`get_current_air_quality`, `calculate_transport_emissions`, `retrieve_environmental_guidelines`, etc.).
- **Structured Grounded Output:** Actionable answers enforce an 8-part schema (*Situation Summary, Verified Conditions, Recommended Action, Why This Helps, Estimated Impact, Uncertainty/Limitations, Safer Alternative, Evidence References*).
- **Streaming UX:** Real-time Server-Sent Events (SSE) with tool-progress indicators, request IDs, and client cancellation propagation.

### Module 6: Trusted Environmental RAG Knowledge Base
- **Curated Corpus:** WHO air quality guidelines, US EPA advisories, CPCB / national public-health guidance, and peer-reviewed environmental literature.
- **Provenance & Freshness:** Every chunk carries publisher, URL, publication/review dates, jurisdiction, language, trust classification, and embedding version. Outdated documents are qualified or retired; citations link directly to ingested sources.

### Module 7: Deterministic Environmental Impact Engine
- **Pure Python Calculators (Zero LLM Math):**
  - *Personal Exposure:* Relative time-weighted exposure comparison (`Concentration × Duration` + infiltration assumptions).
  - *Transportation Emissions:* `Distance (km) × Mode Factor (kg CO2e / passenger-km)` accounting for vehicle occupancy.
  - *Household Energy:* `Power (kW) × Hours × Regional Grid Emission Factor`.
  - *Indoor Filtration:* Room volume + CADR + runtime + ACH effectiveness modeling.
- **Transparency:** Every result sets `is_estimate: true` and lists explicit methodology, assumptions, confidence level, and source references.

### Module 8: Interactive Daily Routine Planner
- **Time-Slotted Output:** Morning, Midday, Afternoon, Evening, and Night blocks with indoor/outdoor tags, exposure notes, safer alternatives, impact estimates, and data timestamps.
- **Priority Hierarchy:** `1. Exposure Safety -> 2. User Schedule Constraints -> 3. Feasibility -> 4. Emission Reduction -> 5. Cost -> 6. Convenience`.
- **User Control:** Regenerate, edit, accept/reject, and save plans—never auto-mutate schedules without confirmation.

### Module 9: Smart Alerts & Digest Engine
- **Triggers:** AQI threshold breach, severe smog episode, forecast deterioration, stale-data warning, cleaner outdoor window, daily digest, and community advisory.
- **Delivery & Safeguards:** In-app, email, and optional web push via Celery + Redis with deduplication, cooldown windows, timezone-aware quiet hours, and one-click opt-out.

### Module 10: Privacy-Preserving Community Module
- **Capabilities:** Neighborhood groups, clean-air challenges (e.g., *No-Idling School Week*, *Public Transit Challenge*), voluntary action logging, aggregate participation & avoided-CO2e dashboards, moderated tips, and abuse reporting.
- **Privacy Guardrail:** Never expose exact household coordinates or non-consented PII; clearly label self-reported actions vs verified ambient measurements.

## 5. End-to-End Core User Journey

1. **Discover:** Guest lands on `/`, selects a city, and views verified current AQI + plain-language health context without forced sign-up or geolocation prompts.
2. **Locate:** User searches a city or explicitly grants geolocation; backend resolves location and surfaces station/model readings with freshness metadata.
3. **Personalize:** User creates an account and optionally configures commute mode, household sensitivity, cooking/ventilation setup, and budget preferences.
4. **Receive Guidance:** Hybrid engine combines live AQI, weather, deterministic safety rules, and RAG guidelines to produce personalized daily cards.
5. **Plan the Day:** User opens `/dashboard/routine-planner`, generates a safer schedule, edits an outdoor workout into an indoor slot during peak smog, and saves the plan.
6. **Compare Alternatives:** User compares solo car vs metro/carpool on `/dashboard/transport`, inspecting both exposure impact and passenger-km CO2e math.
7. **Ask Follow-Ups:** User asks **AirWise Copilot** *"Should I open my windows for 20 minutes right now?"* and receives a streamed, tool-grounded, cited answer.
8. **Stay Protected:** User sets an India NAQI `> 200` alert with 22:00–07:00 quiet hours.
9. **Track Habits:** Logging completed actions updates transparent cumulative CO2e and cost estimates.
10. **Act Together:** User joins a neighborhood *No-Idling School Drop-Off* challenge with aggregated community metrics.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements & Engineering Targets

| Category | Target / Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cached AQI Latency** | `p95 < 300 ms` |
| **Standard Non-AI API Latency** | `p95 < 500 ms` |
| **Copilot Streaming TTFT** | First SSE event `< 3.0 s` under normal provider latency |
| **Accessibility** | WCAG 2.2 Level AA; Lighthouse Accessibility `>= 90`; zero critical axe violations |
| **Internationalization** | `next-intl` locale architecture starting with English (`en`) and structured for Hindi (`hi`) & regional Indian languages |
| **Reliability** | Primary-to-fallback AQI provider failover, circuit breakers, explicit stale-data labeling, deterministic AI fallback |
| **Privacy & Security** | Zero frontend secrets, coarse location passed to LLMs, Argon2id + HttpOnly cookies + CSRF, self-service data export & deletion |
